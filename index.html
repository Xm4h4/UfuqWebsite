FAQ

How do we get started?

Start by sending a short message about what you are building, what feels unclear, and what you want the website or product to achieve. We will review the fit and suggest the next step.

What tools are you using?

We primarily use Figma for interface design, WordPress for editable CMS builds, Framer for focused landing pages, and lightweight project tools for async communication and delivery tracking.

How does async communication work?

Most decisions happen in writing so progress stays clear. We share updates, previews, questions, and next actions without requiring constant meetings.

How fast will I get my design?

Small audits and direction work can move quickly. Full design and WordPress builds depend on scope, but we keep the process in tight review cycles so you can see progress early.

What if I don't like the design?

We work in iterations and explain the reasoning behind decisions. If something misses the mark, we use your feedback to refine the direction before moving deeper into build.
